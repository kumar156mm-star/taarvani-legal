TAARVANI Account Deletion

TAARVANI users can request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from your registered email address.
  2. Use the subject: TAARVANI Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered email address.
  4. We may contact you to verify account ownership.

Request Account Deletion by Email

Data deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account.
  • User profile information.
  • Saved prompts and chat sessions.
  • Daily usage records and associated account data.

Data retention

Account-associated data will be deleted after the request is verified and processed, except where retention is required by applicable law.
